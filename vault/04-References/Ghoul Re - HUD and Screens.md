# Ghoul Re - HUD and Screens

The closest existing game to ours: a Roblox Tokyo Ghoul PvP RPG with ghoul, CCG and Quinx races and permadeath. It has no Fandom-style wiki. Information comes from guide sites and the official Trello and Discord. Screenshots are hotlinked from Sportskeeda.

## In-world HUD
![Ghoul://Re HUD](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2025/03/84586-17429854094265-1920.jpg)

**Layout takeaways**
- Very minimal. One **red health bar** sits bottom centre with a row of small skill or cooldown slots under it.
- A row of small menu icons sits top left, with the shop and settings there too.
- An objective or mission tracker sits on the right side.
- A "Create Squad" button sits bottom left, so party play is one click away.
- NPC and player names float overhead with small portrait icons.
- The scene is dark and foggy, and that does a lot of the mood work. The UI stays out of the way.

## Skill tree
![Ghoul://Re skill tree](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2025/03/16362-17429863105730-1920.jpg)

- Opened with **M** (or the on-screen Menu), then Skill Tree.
- It's a small, dark centred panel with a **red outline**. That matches the series' red and black look.
- Left column: your avatar, race, buffs and debuffs, and a description box that fills in when you hover a skill.
- Four columns of skills: **Strength**, **Dexterity**, **Quinque Mastery**, **Kagune Mastery**. Each skill needs a minimum stat (for example 6 Strength or 35 Dexterity).
- Spare **skill points** show bottom right. Click a skill, then **Learn**.
- These skills are **weaponless** martial moves (kicks, sweeps, uppercuts). They work without a kagune or quinque, which gives humans something to do.

## Progression systems shown in the UI
### Rank (the main progress bar)
Rank is the headline number, not a level. Each rank gives **5 skill points** (Special Class gives 10).

| Ghoul ratings | CCG ranks |
|---|---|
| C → B → B+ → A → A+ → S → S+ → SS → SS+ → SSS | Rank 3 → Rank 2 → Rank 1 → Second Class → Associate First Class → Semi-Associate First Class → First Class → Associate Special → Special Class |

Ranking up needs more than EXP. It also needs **grips** (executions of enemy-faction players, B key), **events attended**, and **permadeaths survived**. Rank gates content: B+ ghouls can eat kagune sacs, and SSS unlocks the kakuja path.

> For us: our [[Threat Ratings]] and [[CCG]] ranks can be the visible "level". Show them big on the stat screen, the way Ghoul://Re does.

### Clans (rolled passive bonus)
- A clan is rolled per character, named after series families (Kaneki, Kirishima, Arima, Suzuya, and so on). Legendary clans have a 3% chance.
- Each gives a small **health** and **max hunger** bonus. Rerolls cost Robux, or you can wipe your character.
- This is the same "rolled at start, Robux reroll" idea as Deepwoken aspects and our kagune roll.

### Equipment
- Up to **6 equipped items**, each of a different type, with stat rolls from 1% to 5%.
