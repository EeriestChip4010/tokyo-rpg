# Note Conventions

## Folders
| Folder | Holds |
|---|---|
| `00-Home` | Entry points and vault rules |
| `01-Lore` | What the Tokyo Ghoul series says. No game mechanics here. |
| `02-Design` | What our game does. Links back to lore notes to justify choices. |
| `03-Roadmap` | Milestones, open questions, decisions |
| `Sources` | Raw research as it was collected, unedited |
| `Templates` | Note templates (Obsidian core Templates plugin) |

## Lore confidence tags
Every lore claim should be traceable. Tag notes or individual lines:
- `#canon`: stated or shown in the manga/anime
- `#verify`: believed true but not yet checked against a source
- `#fanon`: popular fan or wiki interpretation, not clearly canon
- `#game-original`: our invention, deliberately not in the series

When a `#verify` line is checked, swap the tag and add the chapter or wiki link.

## Design status tags
- `#draft`: proposal, open to change
- `#agreed`: decided, build it
- `#cut`: dropped (keep the note so we remember why)

## Numbers
All numbers in design notes are placeholders until playtested. Keep them in tables near the top of each section so they're easy to find and change later.
