# Origins and Race Paths

**Decision:** every new character starts as an ordinary **human** in Tokyo. What they become is earned in-game through routes that follow the series. #agreed

## Lore constraint
In canon, a human can't simply turn into a born ghoul. Humans become ghoul-like only through a **kakuhou transplant**, which makes an artificial one-eyed ghoul like Kaneki (see [[Half-Ghouls and Quinx]]). So player "ghouls" are artificial one-eyed ghouls, which fits: Kaneki is the series' protagonist. #canon

## Paths

### 1. CCG investigator
- Apply at a CCG office and pass an entry test (fitness and a combat trial) to join as an Academy recruit. #game-original
- Graduate to Rank 3 Investigator and get a standard issue quinque. Then follow investigator progression in [[Progression]].
- **Later branch:** Quinx surgery for ranked investigators (see [[Half-Ghouls and Quinx]]).

### 2. Artificial one-eyed ghoul (the Kaneki route)
The route mirrors Kaneki's origin: a near-fatal ghoul attack, then an illegal transplant. #canon basis, #game-original steps
1. **Be nearly killed by a ghoul.** Get downed by a ghoul NPC (or player) at night in a dangerous ward.
2. **Wake up in a back-alley clinic** run by a Kanou-style doctor (an NPC in the series' style, not Kanou himself).
3. **The transplant is a gamble.** There is a chance the body rejects it, which counts as a death under [[Death and Permadeath]]. Higher risk, higher reward.
4. **Survive** and you're a one-eyed ghoul: kakugan in one eye, a rolled kagune, and the hunger mechanic from [[Ghoul Diet]].

Alternative trigger to consider: seeking out the doctor on purpose by finding him through rumors and paying his price. This gives players a deliberate route without needing to get attacked.

### 3. Staying human
- A valid choice: civilians can work, trade, and run the café hub. It's also the starting point for both paths above.
- Humans can't use kagune or quinques, so this is mainly a social or support role.

## Kagune roll
- The kagune type is **rolled randomly** at transplant, framed as the type of the donor ghoul's kakuhou. #agreed
- Proposed rarity (placeholder): Bikaku 35%, Koukaku 30%, Ukaku 25%, Rinkaku 10%. Rinkaku is rarest because it's the series' iconic type (Kaneki, Rize).
- A very rare **chimera** roll (two types) could come later.

## Conflict between paths
- Investigators and ghouls are natural enemies under always-on PvP.
- A ghoul who is exposed (seen using the kakugan or kagune) gains notoriety and a threat rating (see [[Progression#5. Threat rating and notoriety (the unique hook)]]).
- Undercover ghouls in the CCG are possible later: a CCG member who secretly takes the transplant becomes a hidden ghoul investigator. Exposure is risky but makes for great stories.
