# Death and Permadeath

**Decision:** the game uses permadeath. #agreed
The exact rules below are a draft proposal, modelled on Deepwoken's approach.

## Proposal
- Each character has a small number of **lives** (placeholder: 3). Dying costs one.
- When a player is downed they enter a **knocked** state instead of dying instantly. Another player can finish them (kill), carry them, or leave. Being finished costs a life.
- At 0 lives, the character is **wiped**: race, kagune, rank and items are gone. The player starts a new human.
- **What carries over after a wipe** (to soften the loss): codex knowledge, cosmetics, and possibly a small bonus called "legacy points".

## Lore tie-ins
- A ghoul killed by an investigator drops their **kakuhou**, which becomes quinque material (see [[Quinque Design]]). A player's death therefore feeds the game world.
- A failed transplant (see [[Origins and Race Paths]]) costs a life.

## Open
- Can lives be earned back, and how?
- Is there a safe zone (for example the café hub) where players can't be finished?
- How do we stop combat logging (leaving mid-fight to avoid death)?
