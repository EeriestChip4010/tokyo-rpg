# Feeding

How ghouls eat. Lore basis: [[Ghoul Diet]] and [[RC Cells]].

**Decision:** ghouls can feed on downed NPCs and players in two ways. Eating a full body rewards more than eating separate limbs. #agreed

## Two ways to feed
| Option | How | Time | Reward | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Feast** | Eat the whole body where it lies | Long channel, interrupted by any hit | Full hunger refill and the largest RC gain | Safe spot, nobody chasing you |
| **Grab and go** | Tear off a limb and carry it | Quick action | Partial hunger and small RC gain per limb, eaten on the move | Being chased, or the CCG is close |

- A body has a limited number of limbs. Each limb taken lowers what a full feast gives, so the totals stay consistent.
- Eating a **ghoul** body (or limb) also builds the cannibalism value toward [[Kakuja]], as described in [[Progression]].
- Feeding on a downed player counts as finishing them (costs a life, see [[Death and Permadeath]]).

## Presentation
- **Minimal blood particles** only. #agreed
- Limb removal should be stylized: the limb vanishes with a puff of red particles, and the ghoul holds a simple wrapped or shadowed prop. Avoid showing realistic severed limbs, to stay within Roblox content rules.
