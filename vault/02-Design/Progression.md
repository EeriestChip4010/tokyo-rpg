# Progression

Principle: **progress the way the series' characters do.** Ghouls get stronger by eating and evolving their kagune. Investigators get stronger through rank, quinques and experience. No generic "level 1 to 100" bar as the main hook.

## Ghouls

### 1. RC level (core stat)
- Starts around 1,000 (lore range in [[RC Cells]]). It's your max RC pool and scales kagune damage and regeneration.
- Raised mainly by **feeding**: eating human NPC bodies. Each meal gives a small permanent RC gain plus a full refill. #game-original (lore basis: [[Ghoul Diet]])
- Diminishing returns at higher levels, so long-term growth needs harder prey (see Kakuja below).

### 2. Hunger
- A slow-draining meter. Using kagune and healing drains it faster (lore: [[Ghoul Diet]]).
- At low hunger: reduced regeneration. When starving, the [[Kakugan]] shows involuntarily and the player gets brief frenzy surges: more damage, less control. A starving ghoul is visible and dangerous.
- Coffee (at an Anteiku-style café) slows hunger drain but doesn't feed. It's a social hub, not a meal.

### 3. Kagune maturity
Kagune evolve in stages, unlocked by RC level plus a combat milestone:
| Stage | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| Unstable | Basic kagune M1s, 1 skill, kagune occasionally fails to release under stress |
| Stable | 2nd and 3rd skills, reliable release |
| Developed | Shape customization (tail count, crystal pattern) and a signature skill |

### 4. Kakuja path (risk / reward)
- Eating **ghoul** bodies, NPC or player, builds a hidden **cannibalism** value (lore: [[Kakuja]]).
- Thresholds unlock **incomplete kakuja** stages: a partial armor transform with a big power spike.
- Every transform fills a **frenzy** meter. When it's full, the player briefly loses control (auto-attacks the nearest target, can't use the menu). Complete kakuja is endgame and rare.
- This makes cannibalism a real choice: faster power, less control.

### 5. Threat rating and notoriety (the unique hook)
- Every player ghoul gets a **CCG threat rating** (C to SSS, see [[Threat Ratings]]) and, once spotted enough, a **codename** based on their mask and kagune. #game-original
- Rating rises with notoriety: killing investigators, being seen with the kagune out, fighting in busy wards.
- Higher rating gives a higher bounty for investigators and a higher-rated kakuhou drop, but more CCG NPC raids on you.
- Players can wear a **mask**. Fighting unmasked lets witnesses link your face to your codename.

## Investigators (CCG)

### 1. Rank
- Climb the real ranks (Rank 3 to Special Class, see [[CCG]]) through missions: patrols, investigations, raids and ghoul kills.
- Rank unlocks: access to quinque lab tiers, higher-rated quinque crafting, squad leadership.

### 2. Quinques as loot and progression
- Craft quinques from **kakuhou** drops (see [[Quinque Design]]). Better ghouls give better quinques.
- **Familiarity:** the more you use one quinque, the more of its skills you unlock. This pushes players to bond with a weapon, as investigators in the series do.

### 3. Partners
- Pairing with another player (senior and junior partner) gives shared mission rewards and a small buff when near each other (lore: [[CCG#Structure]]).

### 4. Quinx (later)
- High-rank investigators can opt into Quinx surgery: they gain a kagune with **frames**, and releasing frames trades stability for power (lore: [[Half-Ghouls and Quinx]]). Not in the first playable.

## First-playable scope
- Ghoul: RC level, hunger, Unstable to Stable maturity for one kagune type.
- Investigator: Rank 3 to Rank 1, one issue quinque, familiarity.
- Threat rating: display only, with no raids yet.
