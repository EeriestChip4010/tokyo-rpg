# CCG (Commission of Counter Ghoul)

The government organization that hunts ghouls. #canon

## Structure
- HQ in Tokyo, with branch offices for the city's wards. #canon
- Recruits trained at the **Academy** (Juniors). #canon
- Investigators usually work in **pairs**: a senior and a junior partner. #canon
- Research labs make [[Quinques]] from recovered kakuhou. #canon
- **Cochlea** is the CCG's ghoul prison. #canon

## Investigator ranks (low to high) #canon

2. Rank 2 Investigator

4. First Class Investigator
5. Associate Special Class Investigator
6. Special Class Investigator
