# Kakuhou and Kagune

## Kakuhou
- An organ that stores concentrated [[RC Cells]] and releases them to form a kagune. #canon
- Its **position on the body decides the kagune type** (see [[Kagune Types]]). #canon
- It is a ghoul's most valuable organ to the CCG: a kakuhou harvested from a dead ghoul is the raw material for a [[Quinques|quinque]]. #canon
- Transplanting a kakuhou into a human produces an artificial half-ghoul (Kaneki, from Rize's organs) or, with CCG control measures, a Quinx. See [[Half-Ghouls and Quinx]]. #canon

## Kagune
- The predatory organ formed when RC cells burst through the skin and harden. #canon
- It is both weapon and armor, can be reshaped, and regrows after being cut, at the cost of RC cells. #canon
- Kagune vary between individuals in shape, count (for example Rinkaku "tails") and color. #canon
- Kagune strength grows with the ghoul's RC level and with feeding, especially on other ghouls (see [[Kakuja]]). #canon
- Some ghouls have more than one kagune type, usually from cannibalism or experiments. These are often called chimeras. #verify
