# Kakuja

- A mutation from repeatedly eating other ghouls (cannibalism, or "kakuja feeding"). Foreign [[RC Cells]] build up and the kagune grows to cover the body as armor. #canon
- **Incomplete kakuja** are common: partial armor, a mask, or one limb covered. Kaneki's centipede form passes through several stages. #canon
- **Complete kakuja** are rare and extremely powerful, for example Eto's and Yoshimura's (Owl) forms. #canon
- Strong mental cost: kakuja users often show instability, loss of self-control, or madness while transformed. #canon
- Doesn't only come from eating ghouls in a fight. Ghouls who build up cannibalism over time can develop one. #verify
