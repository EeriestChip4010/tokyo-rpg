# Quinques

- CCG weapons made from the [[Kakuhou and Kagune|kakuhou]] of defeated ghouls. #canon
- Carried in a briefcase-like case and deployed in combat. An electrical stimulus from the handle makes the stored RC cells take shape. #canon
- A quinque keeps the **type** of the kagune it came from (Ukaku, Koukaku, Rinkaku or Bikaku quinques), with the matching strengths and weaknesses. See [[Kagune Types]]. #canon
- Quinques are **rated** (for example C up to S) and a quinque's power reflects the ghoul it came from. #verify
- Investigators often name quinques after the ghoul or give them nicknames, and some get attached to them. #canon
- Quinque steel, a related material, is used in other CCG gear such as Q-bullets. #canon

## Notable examples
- **IXA** (Koukaku) and **Narukami** (Ukaku): Kishou Arima #canon
- **13's Jason** (Rinkaku, from Yamori): Juuzou Suzuya #canon
- **Kura**: Kotaro Amon #verify (type)
