# Half-Ghouls and Quinx

## Natural one-eyed ghouls
- Born from a human and a ghoul. Extremely rare and usually very strong (Eto). #canon

## Artificial half-ghouls
- Humans given a ghoul's kakuhou by surgery. Kaneki is the main example, from Rize's organs via Dr. Kanou. #canon
- Kanou's experiments also created other artificial one-eyed ghouls. #canon

## Quinx (Q's)
- CCG investigators with a surgically implanted kakuhou, introduced in *Tokyo Ghoul:re*. #canon
- The implant has **frames** (Quinx frames) that limit RC output. Releasing frames gives more power but risks losing control or self. #canon
- They can use kagune and heal like ghouls, and in some cases can still eat human food. #verify
