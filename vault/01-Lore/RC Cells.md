# RC Cells

RC ("Red Child") cells are the biological basis of everything that makes a ghoul a ghoul. #canon

## What they are
- Cells that circulate in the body like blood. Under a microscope they look like small curled-up fetuses, which is where the "Red Child" name comes from. #canon
- They act like a **liquid muscle**: fluid while circulating, but able to harden into very tough tissue when concentrated and released. #canon
- Hardened RC tissue is what kagune are made of, and it is why a ghoul's skin can break ordinary blades and needles. #canon

## RC level
The **RC level** (RC factor) is the measured concentration of RC cells in the blood.

| Who | Typical RC level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Humans | ~200–500 | Normal, harmless. Humans have no organ to concentrate them. #canon |
| Ghouls | ~1,000–8,000 | High concentration enables kagune, regeneration and strength. #canon |

- CCG uses RC level scans (for example gate scanners at CCG buildings) to detect ghouls. #canon
- A human whose RC level climbs abnormally high starts showing ghoul traits. #verify

## How a ghoul uses them
1. RC cells are concentrated and stored in the [[Kakuhou and Kagune|kakuhou]].
2. Under conscious control, or high stress and excitement, the kakuhou releases them.
3. They break through the skin at a location set by the kakuhou and harden into a [[Kagune Types|kagune]].
4. Rising RC activity also shows in the eyes as the [[Kakugan]].

## Regeneration and strength
- RC cells give ghouls superhuman strength, durability and rapid healing from severe wounds. #canon
- Healing and kagune use both consume RC cells, which are replenished by eating (see [[Ghoul Diet]]). #canon
- Wounds from RC-based weapons (kagune, [[Quinques]], Q-bullets) are what actually threaten a ghoul. Ordinary weapons mostly don't. #canon

## When things go wrong
- **[[Kakuja]]:** eating other ghouls floods the body with foreign RC cells, causing mutation and instability. #canon
- **RC suppressants:** CCG drugs that suppress RC activity, weakening kagune and slowing regeneration. Used on prisoners and in Quinx control. #canon
- **Over-secretion (ROS):** uncontrolled RC growth in humans or hybrids, causing kagune-like growths and mental degradation. #verify (check :re chapters)
- **"RC tubes" formed by injury:** claim that torn muscle is rebuilt as permanent RC pathways that make the user stronger. #verify (not confirmed as canon; may be fanon)
