# Kagune Types

There are four types, set by where the [[Kakuhou and Kagune|kakuhou]] sits. #canon

| Type | Name meaning | Released from | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ukaku** | Feather | Shoulders | Speed, ranged crystal shots | Burns RC fast, poor stamina in long fights |
| **Koukaku** | Shell | Below the shoulder blades | Very dense and hard, great defense | Heavy and slow |
| **Rinkaku** | Scale | Lower back / waist | Huge striking power, fast regeneration | Loosely bonded, brittle, easy to sever |
| **Bikaku** | Tail | Tailbone | Balanced, versatile | No standout strength |

## Matchups
The wiki describes a rock-paper-scissors cycle. #verify
- Ukaku beats Rinkaku
- Rinkaku beats Bikaku
- Bikaku beats Koukaku
- Koukaku beats Ukaku

## Notable examples #canon
- Ukaku: Touka Kirishima
- Koukaku: Hinami Fueguchi (she also has a Rinkaku, making her a chimera) #verify
- Rinkaku: Ken Kaneki, Rize Kamishiro, Yamori (Jason)
- Bikaku: Nishiki Nishio
