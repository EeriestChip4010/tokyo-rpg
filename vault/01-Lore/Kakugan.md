# Kakugan

- A ghoul's "red eyes": black sclera and red iris. #canon
- They appear when RC activity rises: hunger, excitement, fighting, or releasing the kagune. #canon
- Ghouls can usually hide them when calm. Losing control of them is a social danger because it reveals the ghoul. #canon
- Half-ghouls such as Kaneki and natural one-eyed ghouls such as Eto show the kakugan in **one eye only**. #canon
