# Ghoul Diet

- Ghouls can only get nutrition from **human flesh** (and, abnormally, other ghouls). #canon
- Normal human food tastes disgusting to them and they **vomit it up**. They can force it down to blend in, then throw it up later. #canon
- **Coffee and water** are the exceptions. Ghouls can drink both, which is why Anteiku is a café. #canon
- A ghoul needs roughly one human body a month, depending on activity. Using the kagune or healing heavy wounds increases hunger. #verify
- Starving ghouls lose self-control and the kakugan shows involuntarily. #canon
- Some ghouls avoid killing by eating suicide victims. Anteiku collects bodies for this. #canon
- "Gourmets" such as Tsukiyama care about flavor and treat eating as a hobby. #canon
- Eating other ghouls leads toward [[Kakuja]]. #canon
