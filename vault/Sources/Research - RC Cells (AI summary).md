# Research - RC Cells (AI summary)

## Short answer

RC (Red Children) cells are unique, protein-like cells in Tokyo Ghoul that look like curled-up fetuses. They flow through the body like blood, can harden to the strength of teeth, and function as "liquid muscles".

### How they work in ghouls
- **Levels:** Humans normally have low RC cell counts (about 200–500), while ghouls have much higher concentrations (about 1,000–8,000).
- **Storage (Kakuhou):** Ghouls store concentrated RC cells in a sac-like organ called the kakuhou, which acts similarly to a heart by pumping RC cells through vascular-like pathways.
- **Diet:** Ghouls increase and maintain their RC cell supply primarily by consuming human flesh.
- **Kagune formation:** When the kakuhou releases RC cells into the body's vessels (triggered by conscious control or high excitement/stress), the cells pierce through the skin to form a predatory weapon or defense known as the kagune.
- **Regeneration and strength:** RC cells allow ghouls to heal rapidly from severe injuries, grant superhuman physical strength, and make their skin tough enough to break normal metal blades.

### What happens when things go wrong
- **Half-ghouls & RC tubes:** When hybrids or half-ghouls (like Ken Kaneki or the Quinx) are injured, their bodies can form permanent RC cell tubes along damaged muscle paths to heal and reinforce strength.
- **RC Cell Over-Secretion Disease (ROS):** If RC cells multiply uncontrollably or spike abnormally in humans or hybrids, it can cause tumor-like kagune growths and mental/physical degradation.
- **RC suppressants:** The CCG uses chemical RC suppressants to temporarily lower a ghoul's RC activity, weakening their kagune, slowing their regeneration, and making them vulnerable to normal weapons.

## Thorough breakdown

### 1. Basic cellular nature & composition
- **The "liquid muscle":** RC cells function like a hybrid between blood cells and muscle tissue. While floating in the bloodstream, they are fluid. When activated, they can instantly solidify, achieving a structural density that surpasses human bone and rivals high-tensile steel.
- **Physical appearance:** In their micro-state within the blood, they resemble tiny, curled-up human fetuses. When they manifest outside the body, they take on a glowing, vibrant, vascular appearance (usually shades of red, purple, or black).
- **The RC count (the standard metric):** The concentration of RC cells in the body dictates a creature's biological status.
  - Humans: 200 to 500 RC cells. Their bodies lack the organs to concentrate or weaponize them.
  - Ghouls: 1,000 to 8,000+ RC cells. This high concentration is what grants them predatory abilities and superhuman durability.

### 2. The internal network: storage and transport
- **The kakuhou (the storage organ):** Ghouls possess a specialized, sac-like organ called a kakuhou. It acts like a secondary heart or a pressurized reservoir, storing highly concentrated, liquefied RC cells.
  - A ghoul can have one or multiple kakuhou depending on their genetic strength and maturity.
- **RC pathways (vascular system):** RC cells travel from the kakuhou through a specialized network of internal tubes/vessels woven into the ghoul's muscular system.
- **RC tubes (injury adaptation):** When a hybrid or powerful ghoul is repeatedly slashed or has their muscles torn, the body heals by replacing the normal muscle paths with hardened RC tubes. This permanently increases the speed at which RC cells can travel from the storage organ to the extremities, making the ghoul faster, stronger, and more resilient after surviving brutal trauma.

### 3. External manifestation: the kagune
When a ghoul experiences high stress, excitement, or a conscious desire to fight, the kakuhou contracts, pressurizing the RC cells. They are violently pumped through the RC pathways, piercing through the ghoul's skin to form a kagune (their predatory hunting weapon).

The structural behavior of the kagune depends entirely on the location of the kakuhou along the spine, creating four distinct biological archetypes:
- **Ukaku (Shining Feather):** Located around the shoulder blades. The RC cells are released in a highly volatile, gaseous state, allowing them to harden instantly into crystalline wings to shoot hardened spikes.
  - Limitation: Because the cells are released so rapidly, it drains the ghoul's RC reservoir incredibly fast, resulting in terrible stamina.
- **Koukaku (Shining Shell):** Located just below the shoulder blades. The RC cells have an exceptionally high density and rigid structural bonding, forming heavy, metallic-like shields or blades.
  - Limitation: The weight of these highly dense cells makes the ghoul significantly slower and unwieldy.
- **Rinkaku (Shining Scales):** Located around the waist. The RC cells bind loosely together like liquid muscle, forming undulating, tentacle-like structures with immense striking power.
  - Limitation: Because the cellular structure binds loosely to maximize flexibility and rapid cellular regeneration, the kagune itself is structurally brittle and easy to sever.
- **Bikaku (Shining Tail):** Located around the tailbone. The RC cells maintain a perfectly balanced bonding density, creating a tail-like weapon.
  - Limitation: It has no distinct biological weaknesses, but it lacks the extreme offensive, defensive, or speed caps of the other types.

### 4. The dietary loop & cellular maintenance
- **The human flesh requirement:** Ghouls cannot synthesize RC cells naturally; their bodies continuously burn through them to maintain their passive physical superiority. They must consume human flesh because human bodies contain the specific nutrients required to feed and replenish a ghoul's RC cells.
- **The genetic block:** Normal food tastes completely rancid to ghouls because their tongues and digestive enzymes are biologically structured only to process materials high in human RC cell content. Eating normal food degrades a ghoul's physical condition and weakens their RC cell quality.

### 5. Advanced mutations and medical anomalies
- **Cannibalism and the kakuja:** When a ghoul consumes other ghouls, they absorb a massive influx of foreign, highly-concentrated RC cells. This forces their own RC cells to mutate and over-saturate. The result is a kakuja, an armor-like manifestation where the RC cells encase the ghoul's entire body.
  - Side effect: This excessive RC density causes severe neurological strain, often inducing temporary psychosis or permanent madness.
- **ROS Disease (RC Cell Over-Secretion):** A condition that usually affects humans or unstable hybrids. If RC cells multiply out of control, the human body cannot handle the pressure. The cells begin to mutate internally, sprouting tumor-like, painful kagune growths all over the body, eventually causing total mental degradation and organ failure.

### 6. Vulnerabilities and countermeasures
- **RC suppressants:** The CCG (Commission of Counter Ghoul) developed a synthetic chemical compound that blocks the activity of RC cells. When injected or inhaled, it forces the RC cells to liquefy and forces the kakuhou to lock up. This completely prevents a ghoul from manifesting their kagune, drastically slows down their natural regeneration, and reduces their physical durability to human levels.
- **Quinque creation:** Human investigators cannot weaponize RC cells naturally. Instead, they surgically harvest a living kakuhou from a captured/killed ghoul, house it in a mechanized briefcase, and feed it electrical currents to force the stored RC cells to manifest into a controlled weapon.
